# 景观剖面与立面工作流

适用：剖面、立面、竖向关系。默认搭配 `aesthetics/marker-sketch.md`。

明确剖面或立面、切线经过的空间与观察方向。按横向顺序组织道路、活动区、绿地、水体及建筑，表达地形和高差。剖切部分加重或填黑，背景轻描；标高未知时只表达相对关系。

## 英文内容模板
```text
An orthographic landscape {section_or_elevation} through {cut_line_or_view_direction} in a {site_type}. Arrange {spatial_sequence} from left to right. Clearly show {relative_level_changes}, ground profile, {paths_and_structures}, layered planting and {water_edge_if_any}. Use strong poche for cut ground and structures in a section, lighter lines for background elements, and human figures for scale. Reserve an annotation band for verified dimensions and levels.
```

技术约束：默认 16:9，长剖面可加宽；保持正投影。缺少实测资料时不生成精确工程标高或构造节点。

负面词：`perspective view, impossible grading, floating foundations, mismatched water levels, unreadable dimensions, inconsistent tree scale`。

检查：地面连续、高差合理、剖切与背景有区分、人物和乔灌草比例一致。
