# 手绘透视与鸟瞰工作流

适用：人视点、鸟瞰、景观效果图。搭配所选 `aesthetics/` 风格前缀。

先选人视点或鸟瞰；缺省按主文件使用人视点。人视点可用约 1.6 m 视高和自然广角；鸟瞰说明观察方向和俯视程度。组织前景植物、中景活动和后景树木或建筑。

## 英文内容模板
```text
A {eye_level_or_aerial} landscape perspective of a {site_type}, approximately {site_dimensions}, viewed from {viewpoint_and_direction}. Feature {main_spatial_focus}, {paths_and_paving}, {seating_and_structures}, and {water_features_if_any}. Layer foreground groundcover and shrubs, midground people and activity spaces, and background tree canopies and contextual buildings. Show {season} vegetation in {time_of_day} light, with consistent perspective, a coherent horizon and believable human scale.
```

技术约束：默认 16:9 横幅；人视点人物头部与视平线关系应符合各自地面标高；避免夸张镜头畸变。

负面词：`fisheye distortion, conflicting vanishing points, oversized people, floating benches, impossible stairs, duplicated trees, inconsistent shadows`。

检查：透视统一、层次清晰、人物与设施比例合理、植物不完全重复、水体倒影方向正确。
