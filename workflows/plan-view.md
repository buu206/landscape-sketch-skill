# 彩色平面图工作流

适用：彩平、总平面、方案平面。搭配所选 `aesthetics/` 风格前缀。

先明确场地边界、长宽、出入口、功能分区、道路、植物和水体。采用正投影俯视，保持道路、树冠与建筑尺度关系。朝向未知时说明概念假设。

## 英文内容模板
```text
Orthographic top-down landscape concept plan of a {site_type}, approximately {width} by {length} meters. Show {site_boundary}, {entrances}, {program_zones}, connected {paths}, {paving_materials}, layered tree canopies, shrub beds and groundcover, and {water_features_if_any}. Maintain readable circulation and coherent spatial proportions. Reserve clean margins for a north arrow, a scale bar and a compact legend. Consistent shadows cast toward the lower right under an assumed upper-left light source.
```

技术约束：默认 4:3 横幅，可按场地长宽调整；正投影、轮廓清晰、功能可辨。尺度仅作概念表达，标注须复核。

负面词：`oblique perspective, warped boundary, disconnected paths, inconsistent scale, illegible labels, conflicting shadows`。

检查：边界闭合、出入口连通、树冠不遮没主要空间、指北针与比例尺一致。模型文字不可靠时预留标注位置，并给出后期标注清单。
