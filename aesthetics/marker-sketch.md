# 马克笔手绘风格

用于彩平、透视及剖立面。将以下前缀置于工作流内容模板之前。

```text
Professional landscape architectural marker sketch on clean white paper, confident fine ink outlines, varied line weights, translucent alcohol-marker color washes, fresh natural greens and warm neutral paving tones, selective hatching, expressive yet controlled strokes, clear spatial hierarchy and restrained detail.
```

调整：用笔触密度和饱和度调节表现强度，重点稍重、背景轻描。彩平保持树冠与材质可辨；剖面强化剖切线；透视保留景深层次。

风格负面词：`muddy colors, heavy black outlines everywhere, excessive crosshatching, glossy plastic surfaces, chaotic scribbles`。
