# 写实景观渲染风格

主要用于透视、鸟瞰和季节氛围；写实彩平仍保持正投影。将以下前缀置于工作流内容模板之前。

```text
High-quality realistic landscape architectural visualization, physically plausible daylight, natural material textures, botanically believable layered planting, realistic human scale, subtle surface variation, coherent shadows and reflections, balanced exposure and restrained color grading.
```

调整：按季节与时段改变色温、阴影和植物状态；铺装纹理服从尺度，避免反光过强。近景精细、远景适度简化，建筑及地形几何保持稳定。

风格负面词：`plastic foliage, repeated cloned plants, excessive bloom, oversharpening, distorted geometry, impossible reflections, overexposed highlights`。
