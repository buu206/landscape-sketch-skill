# 水彩淡彩风格

用于彩平、透视和季节氛围。将以下前缀置于工作流内容模板之前。

```text
Refined landscape architectural watercolor illustration, delicate ink linework, transparent layered washes, subtle paper grain, soft pigment transitions, fresh botanical colors, reserved white highlights, atmospheric depth and a calm, balanced composition.
```

调整：微调透明度、冷暖及纸张纹理。边缘可柔化，但道路、边界和空间结构保持可辨。四季对比保持统一笔触与纸张质感。

风格负面词：`opaque muddy paint, oversaturated colors, uncontrolled bleeding, blurred spatial boundaries, excessive paper texture`。
